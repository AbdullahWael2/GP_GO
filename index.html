The idea

Your phone dies. The fix should be on the shelf.

Everyone has been stuck at 3% away from home. Most fixes only work if you planned ahead. GP GO is for the times you didn't.

Today

Low battery, no plan

  • Your power bank is at home, or it's empty too.
  • You need the right cable and somewhere to plug in.
  • Charging spots mean waiting in one place.
With GP GO

Charged and in your hand

  • Sold ready to use, at the counter where you already are.
  • Connector built in. Plug it straight into your phone.
  • Keep moving while it charges. Use it once and you're done.
How it works

Three steps. About three seconds.

  1. Low battery?

    Grab

    Pick up a GP GO at the checkout of a gas station, supermarket or store near you.

  2. Open the pack

    Plug

    Plug it straight into your phone. The USB-C connector is built in.

  3. Back to life

    Go

    Put your phone back in your pocket and carry on. Your battery fills up while you move.

About us

Two friends from Jeddah.

We started GP GO because we kept running into the same problem: a dead phone at the worst moment, and no quick way to fix it.

We're building a Saudi brand around one simple product and doing it properly. That means a vetted manufacturer, Saudi conformity certification, recognized battery safety testing, and Arabic labeling on every pack before anything reaches a shelf.

Our approach is practical: keep the product simple, make it safe, and put it where people actually need it.
